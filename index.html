About 关于我们

Founded in 2013, the Oxford China Forum (OCF) is a student-led academic society at the University of Oxford. It has grown into one of the largest and most academically rigorous China-focused forums in the United Kingdom. OCF brings together leading experts on China-related issues and Chinese and international students from across the UK for in-depth discussion of a rapidly changing China. Previous editions of the Forum have been livestreamed globally on platforms including Sohu, Douyin and Phoenix New Media, reaching a cumulative audience of more than 10 million views.

Over thirteen editions we have welcomed more than 300 distinguished guests, including former UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon, former Prime Minister of Australia Kevin Rudd, Sir Vince Cable, former UK Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills, and Harvard political philosopher Michael Sandel.

牛津中国论坛（OCF）成立于 2013 年，是牛津大学的学生学术组织，如今已发展成全英规模最大、最为严谨学术的中国论坛之一。OCF 致力于汇集中国话题的领域行家，与来自英国各地的中外学生，共同对变化中的中国进行深度讨论。往届嘉宾包括前联合国秘书长潘基文、澳大利亚前总理陆克文、英国前商务部大臣文斯·凯布尔爵士、哈佛大学政治哲学家迈克尔·桑德尔等 300 余位。

Annual Forum 年度论坛

Our flagship event is the Annual Forum, held each March at Saïd Business School, with a keynote session and several themed panels. Alongside it we run a summer forum and smaller panels and socials during term.
